In his 2018 Design in Tech Report, John Maeda made the case for the computational designer, one who designs for scale. As he put it, "execution has outpaced innovation." The question of what to build and why is one designers are in a unique position to answer.

I studied graphic design at ArtCenter, where we learned print down to the grain of the paper stock. Once I started working, I didn’t print a single thing. My designs lived on screens, shaped by code and algorithms I didn’t fully understand. So I went to Harvard for a master’s in data science to learn that medium. Today I’m most interested in computer vision and design engineering.
